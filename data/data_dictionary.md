## Derived Metrics for Data Warehouse & Mining

During ETL transformation, the following analytical measures and flags are computed:

1. **`Discount_Amount_INR`**:  
   $$\text{Discount\_Amount} = (\text{Unit\_Price\_INR} \times \text{Quantity}) \times \left(\frac{\text{Discount\_Percent}}{100}\right)$$

2. **`Line_Total_INR`**:  
   $$\text{Line\_Total} = (\text{Unit\_Price\_INR} \times \text{Quantity}) - \text{Discount\_Amount\_INR}$$

3. **`Is_Weekend`**:  
   Boolean flag (`True` if Day is Saturday or Sunday, else `False`) to model weekend grocery rush patterns.

4. **`Time_Slot`**:  
   Categorical bucket (`Morning: 09:00-12:00`, `Afternoon: 12:00-16:00`, `Evening: 16:00-20:00`, `Night: 20:00-22:00`).

5. **`Basket_Value_Category`**:  
   Classification target: `High Spender` (Basket Total $\ge$ ₹1,000) vs `Budget Spender` (Basket Total < ₹1,000).
